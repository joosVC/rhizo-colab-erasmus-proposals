# Official discovery map

Select sources for the actual dossier and sector. This is a discovery map, not a requirement to search every site or cite every institution. Verify the actual pages, documents and dates used at execution; inclusion here is not a claim that each portal or all its content was inspected.

| Purpose | Entry points | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Erasmus+ call | [Guide and versions](https://erasmus-plus.ec.europa.eu/erasmus-programme-guide), [document library](https://erasmus-plus.ec.europa.eu/resources-and-tools/documents-and-guidelines) | Exact action/priority text and current versions; use the year-specific PDF |
| Adopted policy/status | [EUR-Lex](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/), [Council](https://www.consilium.europa.eu/) | Original recommendations, conclusions and decision metadata; follow news to text |
| Education policy | [European Education Area](https://education.ec.europa.eu/), [document library](https://education.ec.europa.eu/resources-and-tools/documents) | School/HE and cross-cutting strategies, frameworks, reports and announcements |
| Skills/VET | [Cedefop publications](https://www.cedefop.europa.eu/en/publications), [DG EMPL](https://employment-social-affairs.ec.europa.eu/) | VET, skills, labour-market relevance, learning outcomes and implementation |
| Research/data | [JRC](https://joint-research-centre.ec.europa.eu/), [Eurostat](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/), [Eurydice](https://eurydice.eacea.ec.europa.eu/) | Frameworks, datasets and comparative education-system evidence |
| Agency context | [Epos](https://www.epos-vlaanderen.be/), [Erasmus+ Netherlands](https://www.erasmusplus.nl/), [agency directory](https://erasmus-plus.ec.europa.eu/contacts/national-agencies) | Choose the applicant jurisdiction and action; verify call-specific national emphasis |
| International context | [OECD education](https://www.oecd.org/en/topics/education.html), [UNESCO education](https://www.unesco.org/en/education) | Complementary frameworks/evidence, not EU grant rules |
| Planned EU initiatives | [Commission Work Programme](https://commission.europa.eu/strategy-and-policy/strategy-documents/commission-work-programme_en) | Horizon context, distinct from the Erasmus+ Annual Work Programme |

## Search examples

Use terms derived from the input, not a fixed topic checklist:

- `site:education.ec.europa.eu [challenge] [year] recommendation framework`
- `site:eur-lex.europa.eu [theme] Council conclusions recommendation [year]`
- `site:cedefop.europa.eu [VET theme] policy [year]`
- `[relevant agency] [call year] KA210/KA220 European priorities national context`

Try synonyms and institutional terminology. Search filters discover leads; verify source dates and status in the document. A general EU digital/green policy need only enter if the link to education and this dossier is explicit. Do not infer latest status from a search engine's “published/crawled” label.
