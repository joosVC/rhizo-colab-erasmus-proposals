# RHIZO KA2 project architecture

## Default objective functions

Write one general objective from desired impact, then these four project-specific SO functions in order:

| ID | Function | Design question |
| --- | --- | --- |
| SO1 | Knowledge sharing and knowledge development | What can partners understand, adapt or design together that they cannot yet do adequately? |
| SO2 | Capacity building through training and piloting | What can intended users apply competently in practice, with what support? |
| SO3 | Sustainable use and scaling | What will keep the practice in use and enable credible transfer beyond the initial group? |
| SO4 | Dissemination | Which relevant groups will encounter and engage with usable results through appropriate channels? |

These are RHIZO's design defaults, not four mandatory EU objectives. Replace generic headings with concrete changes in the chosen topic. Explain a deliberate departure. Do not confuse dissemination reach with adoption or sustained use.

## Results mean shareable deliverables

Use R1, R2… for the few large products the partnership develops and can share. Use “outcome” for changes in people/practice and “activity” for what partners do. Keep this vocabulary even if a source uses “results” more broadly.

Candidate result families, selected only when they serve the mechanism:

- Evidence-informed framework or practice handbook.
- Teacher training/facilitation kit or reusable didactic units.
- Learner-facing guide, tutorials or practice resources.
- Implementation/activation kit for schools or networks.
- Implementation playbook with cases and transfer conditions; call it validated only when the design and evidence support that word.
- Reusable e-course/MOOC, advocacy kit, manifesto or policy recommendations where there is a concrete user and uptake pathway.
- Curated ambassador/case-story resource where it enables transfer rather than merely publicity.

A training kit may contain slides, facilitator notes and worksheets; those are components, not three extra results. A local pilot or training event is an activity. Its documented transferable method can be a result if worth developing and sharing. A website hosting results is normally a dissemination channel. Do not create R-numbers for SO4 events, campaigns, routine social media, meetings or management. A substantive policy/advocacy resource under SO3 may support SO4 without making dissemination an extra product portfolio.

Normally align the result count with core knowledge partners: one substantial lead result per core organisation, not per capability or per implementation school. Prefer two meaningful results for two capable core partners over five artificial ones. An implementation partner may lead a substantive result if explicitly justified. If useful outputs and partner count conflict, revisit the design; do not divide one kit into fragments just to satisfy the heuristic.

## Work packages and mappings

For KA220 use WP1 for management. It supports all work and has no artificial educational SO. Its coordination/evaluation tasks still require a proportionate approach in a later application. Group the other work into coherent development, capacity-building/application, embedding and dissemination functions. A WP can contain several results and support several SOs. A result may contribute to more than one SO; give it one clear accountable lead rather than ambiguous joint ownership.

Keep a compact mapping with SO, WP or activity cluster, R IDs, enabling activities and lead. Include SO4 even though it has no new R. Do not force one WP per result or create a WP merely to give each partner a title. At concept stage show phases/dependencies without a detailed GANTT.

For KA210 retain the same SO/result logic with simpler activity clusters. Do not imply KA220's formal management WP/budget cap is mandatory for KA210. Make the expertise exchange and mutual benefits clear even when there are only two partners.

Describe how each result will be co-developed, tested, revised and used. Explain how knowledge partners learn from one another and implementation sites; schools are contributors of practice knowledge, not merely test subjects. Specify likely users, adoption support and proposed continuing ownership, with commitments marked unconfirmed where needed.
