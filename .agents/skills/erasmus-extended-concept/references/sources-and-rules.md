## How to check formal rules during use

Record the target year, action, field, applicant country/agency, source version and relevant passage. Use a supplied authoritative call document where available; verify updates selectively when access permits. Do not treat this dated locator or a previous policy scan as a permanently verified corpus.

Check only rules decisive for the proposed architecture: appropriate action/field, relevant accreditation, scale/partner minima, activity eligibility and durations/count restrictions, funding model and any allocation cap being invoked. Do not reproduce the entire Guide or create a long administration checklist. Read the right SCH/VET section rather than extrapolating from adult education. Select the relevant EPOS documents by action, field and year, not every library entry.

For an unpublished target call, label the prior-year baseline and resulting eligibility uncertainty. With no browsing or supplied relevant text, draft the pedagogical/cooperation design provisionally and state that eligibility was not verified. Never report a source as read if only its title or search result was available.

## 2026 baseline inspected on 2026-10-15

These summaries are orientation only; recheck for a real dossier and later calls.

- [KA220 Cooperation Partnerships](https://erasmus-plus.ec.europa.eu/programme-guide/part-b/key-action-2/cooperation-partnerships), eligibility and funding sections: at least 3 organisations in 3 eligible programme countries; €120,000/€250,000/€400,000; management WP with at most 20% of the grant, and up to 4 implementation WPs recommended. Recommendation is not a hard maximum. A funded university's ECHE and other relevant eligibility conditions need checking. Associated partners receive no programme funding.
- [KA210 Small-scale Partnerships](https://erasmus-plus.ec.europa.eu/programme-guide/part-b/key-action-2/small-scale-partnerships), eligibility/funding: at least 2 organisations in 2 eligible programme countries; €30,000 or €60,000, with simpler activity-based design. RHIZO's €60,000 preference is internal. The action is not a KA210-HED funding route; a university participating in a school/VET partnership does not change its field.
- [KA1 school education](https://erasmus-plus.ec.europa.eu/programme-guide/part-b/key-action-1/school-pupils-and-staff) and [KA1 VET](https://erasmus-plus.ec.europa.eu/programme-guide/part-b/key-action-1/mobility-vet), short-term eligibility/scope: 6–18 months, no same-field accreditation, at most 30 mobility participants excluding accompanying persons and preparatory visits; at least one staff/learner mobility required. The 12-month preference is a RHIZO design choice. Check the proposed format's specific limits; there is no single general maximum number of trips.
- Staff activity restrictions in both KA1 sections include at most two participants per shadowed mentor simultaneously, at most three from the sending organisation attending the same course together, and one course per person/project. Course-related grant caps and format durations also need checking when relevant; do not assume every permitted format is feasible within school capacity.
- [EPOS KA1 library](https://www.epos-vlaanderen.be/bibliotheek-categorie/erasmus-ka1/) and [2026 webinar materials](https://www.epos-vlaanderen.be/bibliotheek-categorie/2026-webinars/): KA122 call information, quality standards and Projectkompas support; learning programme/agreement templates become useful later. The KA122 Call 2026 infosession and quality-standards presentation were read during design; other listed items were identified, not all read.

The repository's [policy register](../../../../policy-sources/README.md) helps locate Guide/AWP versions and amendments. Recent policy priorities belong to the policy-scan phase; this skill checks the few formal rules that affect its design. A network partner, equal core budgets, one lead result per core partner and pilot cohort ranges are RHIZO heuristics, not EU eligibility rules.
