The public interactive examples linked through toc.erasmusplus.nl were discovered but their JavaScript content was not readable through text retrieval; they are not the evidential basis for worked examples in this skill. The bundled illustrations are RHIZO-authored synthetic design patterns.

The RHIZO adaptation adds alternative scenario cards, claim provenance, explicit uncertainty, mechanism/assumption notes and a team decision handoff. Its three KA2 functions are a reusable organisational design pattern, not official prescribed WPs. No private proposal text or planning spreadsheet is reproduced.

Use only the underlying current call documents to establish formal requirements. These web URLs can change content; the method stays independent of a fixed call year. Do not hard-code amounts, deadlines, participant minima or funding percentages from them into a provisional impact scenario.

Keep the National Agency's contribution logic while avoiding absolute claims about universal outcome timing or inability to observe long-term change. The early pack need not complete every monitoring or stakeholder field. It should remain sufficient to make a reasoned team choice.
