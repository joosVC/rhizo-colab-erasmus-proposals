# Method and product sources

Checked 2026-10-14. These sources support the design, not claims about particular interventions. Recheck product instructions when maintaining the skill; ordinary prompt preparation needs no browsing.

## Question framing

- [JBI Manual, defining and aligning objectives and questions](https://jbi-global.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/MANUAL/pages/355862687): population/concept/context and coherent subquestions for mapping a landscape; other question structures where appropriate. Use this as a framing aid. This skill does not implement a JBI scoping review or establish systematic completeness.
- [EEF, review of evidence on implementation in education](https://educationendowmentfoundation.org.uk/education-evidence/evidence-reviews/implementation-in-education): selection and implementation in educational contexts motivate asking for applicability and conditions alongside effects. Do not treat this reference as evidence for an unrelated candidate intervention.

## Elicit workflow

- [Which Elicit workflow should I use?](https://support.elicit.com/en/articles/14757543-getting-started-with-elicit-which-workflow-should-i-use): Research Agent, Research Report and Systematic Review serve different needs. Reports provide topic overviews; systematic reviews offer more screening/extraction control.
- [Generate a Research Report](https://support.elicit.com/en/articles/14756862-get-a-research-report-to-generate-in-depth-answers-automatically-in-elicit): a precise question informs search/screening/extraction; users can review generated stages and export available source data. A requested layout is not assured or a substitute for checking the results.
- [Elicit API reference](https://docs.elicit.com/): optional future integration; no execution dependency of this prompt-only skill.
- [Elicit API terms](https://elicit.com/operations/api-terms): relevant only to any separately designed integration and account-sharing arrangement. Do not hardcode an assumed entitlement or personal-key distribution into this skill.

## RHIZO design choices

The two-track established/emerging search, bounded adjacent-field exploration, flexible shortlist and science/policy → provisional impact scenarios → team choice/refinement → PRP sequence are agreed project-development choices. They are not mandatory Erasmus+ assessment criteria or guarantees of funding. Novelty, evidence confidence, adoption and contextual relevance are separate judgments.
