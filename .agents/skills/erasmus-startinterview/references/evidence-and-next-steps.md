# Evidence and next steps

## Starting point

Describe one or more origins: school need, personal/professional interest, proposed approach, transfer from another project, or mixture. Separate the desired change from a preferred mechanism. A wish for less disengagement plus an outdoor activity does not establish the cause or the activity's effect. Do not enlarge the ambition merely to sound European.

## Evidence discipline

Use labels selectively; attribution in prose can suffice.

| Label | Meaning | Reporting rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Ervaring** | Participant-described episode or pattern | Write ‘De docent beschrijft…’; do not imply independent observation or universal prevalence. |
| **Vermoeden** | Interpretation, explanation, hoped-for effect, tentative idea | Retain attribution and uncertainty; do not convert correlation to cause or hope to outcome. |
| **Open vraag** | Missing knowledge or an unexplored topic | Distinguish ‘Nog onbekend’ from ‘Niet besproken’. |
| **Te verifiëren** | Mentioned policy, study, statistic, project, approval, or effect claim | Preserve supplied titles/links as leads; do not invent references or browse during intake. |

For supplied documents, identify which document asserts what. Polished AI-generated input is not stronger evidence than a rough spoken account. Two colleagues agreeing does not establish a whole-school priority. Quote only exact available wording; paraphrase unclear dictation. Remove incidental names and identifying pupil information from the shareable brief.

## Proportionate recommendation

Give one primary route, its reason, a useful action, and when to revisit. Keep any secondary actions in order.

| Route | Choose when | Action and condition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Eerst intern afstemmen** | The next exploration or implementation depends on school time, staff, decisions, or resources, and relevant support is unclear | Share the note with the relevant colleague, coordinator, or leadership role. Ask what they recognise and what space is available. Revisit when needed support is clearer. |
| **Eerst het startpunt verduidelijken** | The account remains generic or lacks available practice context needed to formulate useful research questions | Collect a few real experiences, existing records, or affected people's perspectives. Specify the gap. If it needs research expertise or the teacher cannot realistically resolve it, recommend Joos rather than blocking. |
| **Gesprek met Joos voorbereiden** | The account and school connection are sufficiently clear, and the next uncertainty needs design or research expertise | Bring the note and priority questions. A validated intervention, quantitative need, partner, or call choice is not required. |

Support is proportional: one person can explore an interest; school-wide changes need relevant people involved. Unknown support is not resistance. Leadership sponsorship is not evidence of learner need. Do not require everyone to agree to a small exploration.

Recommend temporary parking only for an input-grounded obstacle, such as no capacity this year, explicit refusal of necessary school involvement, or acknowledged priority mismatch. State the reason and a reopening condition. Do not judge eligibility or predict funding. If the route cannot be chosen reliably, recommend one clarification instead of a confident verdict.

## Research agenda

Select up to three questions that unlock the next decision. State what is uncertain, why it matters, and a proportionate future method/source. Possible directions: shared extent of the experience, causes/barriers, existing approaches, or verification of a mentioned policy/project. Suggest internal conversations, existing aggregated information, observation, literature review, or policy verification as later work, never as already completed.

Urgency, novelty, impact potential, transferability, scalability, and policy momentum can become research questions. Do not score them or force evidence the teacher lacks. A familiar problem can still warrant exploration. If a mechanism is supplied, retain only a tentative chain at its actual certainty level; omit it if absent. Never complete a Theory of Change to fill a template.

## Invitation and handoff

Invite concrete contributions: a contrasting classroom episode, existing initiative, school-priority clarification, or practical support. Use supplied or generic roles. Do not assign a named colleague a task without their involvement. Be supportive without presenting effects as proven, fabricating urgency, or implying EU funding. Preserve minority views and openness to additions.

Leave action, sector, partners, lump sum, policy fit, solution design, and English extended concept writing for the later human discussion and dedicated workflows. Recommend a task type, not an unbuilt callable skill. Do not automatically start it. For an explicit task change, acknowledge the new phase and keep new evidence distinct from the interview account.
