# Method sources and design choices

Sources consulted on 13 October 2026. These support the interview method; they do not validate any school need, intervention, or Erasmus+ eligibility. Do not browse them during the participant intake.

| Source | Principle used |
| --- | --- |
| [GOV.UK — Using in-depth interviews](https://www.gov.uk/service-manual/user-research/using-in-depth-interviews) | Start with accessible questions; listen to real stories; use neutral follow-ups; adjust to the participant; close with clarifications and explain the next step. |
| [Nielsen Norman Group — Writing an Effective Guide for a UX Interview](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/interview-guide/) | Use a semi-structured guide rather than a rigid script; ask open questions about specific episodes; introduce reflective questions after experiences have been recalled; pilot the guide. |
| [IDEO.org Design Kit — Interview](https://www.designkit.org/methods/2.html) | Hear participants in their own words; move from broader context to specific experiences; retain what was said rather than replacing it with the interviewer's interpretation. |
| [IDEO.org Design Kit — Group Interview](https://www.designkit.org/methods/group-interview.html) | Invite multiple voices and quieter participants; preserve differences rather than assuming the loudest voice represents everyone. |
| [OpenAI — Build skills](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/build-skills) | Use precise trigger metadata, concise instructions, progressive loading of resources, and a standard skill folder. |
| [OpenAI — Build skills for plugins](https://developers.openai.com/plugins/build/skills) | Keep one recognisable user goal per skill; make boundaries, output and unknowns explicit; test representative and incomplete requests; instruction-only workflows need no MCP server. |

## RHIZO-specific choices

The following are product decisions from the project-owner conversation, not universal research prescriptions:

- Recommend speech/dictation; retain text as an equally valid fallback.
- Suggest 15–20 minutes; accept ten minutes or extensions; ask about availability rather than inventing a timer.
- Capture starting knowledge and unknowns before external research.
- Use English authoring instructions, Dutch conversation and internal sharing output.
- Deliver a starting note and research agenda, not a compulsory Theory of Change.
- Recommend proportionate internal alignment, clarification, or discussion with Joos.
- Preserve policy, partner, action and budget decisions for subsequent work.
- Use an invitation to colleagues as part of the output without treating unknown support as opposition.

## Limits of the adaptation

This is an initial project-owner/staff interview. It is not a representative needs survey, direct evidence of pupils' experiences, observation of classroom behaviour, or assessment of intervention effectiveness. Claims about pupils remain second-hand unless they supplied their own account. A short AI interview also lacks contextual observation and may receive only transcribed text. Keep these limits reflected in report wording rather than making participants complete a methodological checklist.
