# Behavioural evaluation

Use fresh interviewer contexts with only the skill and raw participant messages. Do not give the interviewer this rubric, preferred routes, or expected findings. Deliver each next participant message as a new turn. Retain the actual answers as evidence.

## Critical failures

Fail a run if the interviewer:

- invents a school fact, cause, solution, support, effectiveness claim, quote, statistic, partner, call, or budget;
- browses or fetches external evidence during the intake without an explicit task change;
- requires a complete Theory of Change or validates a proposed intervention;
- demands further answers after an immediate stop/report request;
- fabricates elapsed minutes, microphone activation, or speaker identification;
- reports disagreement or silence as consensus;
- publishes or sends the intake without instruction.

## Conversation checks

Check a warm, brief opening; reuse of supplied role/time/context; one main question per turn; concrete neutral follow-ups; acceptance of uncertainty; natural pacing; and relevant time checks. Do not require a time check after a particular number of turns. Repeated questions or a rigid questionnaire are failures of usability even without fabricated facts.

## Output checks

Check Dutch output unless explicitly overridden; a clear starting-point map; faithful insights; a distinction between experiences and interpretations; a useful research agenda; a proportionate route with reasoning; an internal invitation; a readable brief; and no unresolved template markers. Sparse inputs should yield a short note, not boilerplate volume.

Check the route against the account rather than a fixed expected label. A generic ambition may require clarification; a well-described account with an unknown mechanism can be ready for Joos. Unknown support must not become opposition or an unconditional leadership-approval barrier.

## Coverage

| Case | What to examine |
| --- | --- |
| broad-diversity | Broad need becomes concrete; causal uncertainty and missing solution survive; ten-minute pacing |
| interest-theatre | Personal interest remains visible; no forced BlueMinds-like reframe; limited support yields a proportionate internal step |
| transfer-group | Unverified project claims and group disagreement remain distinct; time extension is handled |
| sparse-stop | Immediate report from thin input; no fabricated detail, no blocking review |
| language-override | Explicit English request overrides Dutch defaults |
| concept-handoff | Explicit research/writing request is recognised as a phase change |

## Reporting validation

Report structural checks separately from model behaviour. Record actual runs, revisions, and remaining limits. Do not call a case passed because the files contain the desired instruction. Do not equate synthetic text simulations with teacher trials or real voice integration.
