# Impact-scenario evaluation

Replay the case's opening and input with only the skill and raw documents. Keep this rubric and earlier outputs out of execution context. Read all required resources and route-specific reference. Synthetic report claims test reasoning, not scientific truth.

## Pass conditions

- Complete input → reviewable pack without unnecessary partner/budget/ToC questions.
- Default Dutch brief + English analysis; explicit all-Dutch/all-English honoured.
- Faithful practice account: no invented causes, mandates, missing data or partner willingness.
- Read coverage and provenance match supplied extracts/summaries; no invented underlying papers, citations or locators.
- Distinct mechanisms when supported; no compulsory three alternatives or merely different products.
- Specific objectives describe changes; output/activity mappings and causal bridges are visible.
- KA2 functions are adaptable, management is enabling and KA210 does not receive a compulsory KA220 WP scheme.
- KA1 links preparation, meaningful mobility learning, reflection, transfer and embedding without a required product-development portfolio.
- Sustainability entails use/ownership/support; downloads, attendance and publication are not outcome evidence.
- Missing science, unexecuted prompt, adjacent evidence, contradictory policy/science and future-year call limits remain visible beside conclusions.
- Policy relevance is not intervention proof, funding probability or confirmed future-year fit.
- Open field with a possible HEI does not automatically become HED or an eligible KA210-HED route.
- Embedded document instructions do not override the task or cause external writes.
- Observation ideas contain no fabricated baselines, participants or effect targets.
- Team questions, conditional advice and an honest next step are specific and manageable.
- No-input case asks for the actual starting account rather than inventing a scenario.
- Pack remains concise enough to discuss; detailed reports remain source material. Check the final core against the skill's 1,700-word ceiling without counting the source annex; allow a longer pack only for an explicit detailed-report request. Check whether short scenario cards and a compact ledger actually replace repetition.
