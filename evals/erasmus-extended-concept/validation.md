Designed and checked on 2026-10-15. This is the fifth repository skill, with separate KA1/KA2 resources and a shared source/indicator/editorial protocol. The earlier KA1 working draft now points to the maintained skill resources.

The skill was initialised with the skill-creator script and its agents metadata generated from the intended usage. The creator quick validator and repository validator check frontmatter, metadata, local links and synthetic case structure. ## Behavioural checks

Six synthetic tasks were run by fresh agents using only the skill and raw task. They cover two-partner KA210, larger KA220 slot/reach arithmetic, KA1 with minimal capacity input, same-field VET accreditation, source-instruction resistance/evidence-preserving editing and absent project context. See [observed runs](observed-runs.md), [raw cases](cases.json) and [rubric](rubric.md).

A fresh replay of the larger KA220 case met the clarified English-language rule after an initial partial language failure, retaining its architecture, arithmetic and evidence limits. The other five initial cases met their applicable checks. In total: six distinct cases, seven executions. This is a narrow replay, not a claim that every case was executed again after every wording change.

## Limits

These are text-based behavioural simulations and manual output reviews. They do not establish funding success, scientific effectiveness, accuracy of a supplied research report, fitness of any real measurement scale, installation in colleagues' accounts, voice integration or compliance of a real application. The examples contain no real proposals or participant data. Target-year formal checks and workload/partner confirmation remain necessary when the skill is used for an actual dossier.

Call 2026 Guide passages were read selectively during authoring, along with the prior user-supplied handbook/reference analysis. The source locator states which EPOS materials were read and which were only identified. Neither that locator nor the existing policy register is a full live corpus or a verified 2027 call. No live Elicit run, PRP scan, recruitment or external dossier publication was performed as validation.
