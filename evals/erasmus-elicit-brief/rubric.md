# Behavioural evaluation

Give execution agents only the installed skill resources and the raw case opening, then any user turns. Keep this rubric and other agents' outputs out of their context. Capture actual outputs; do not replace them with ideal examples.

## Pass conditions

- Use existing input without restarting the interview; draft immediately for adequate/proceed-now cases. Request only the missing note/account when absent.
- Preserve local uncertainty and source attribution; no invented ages, causes, support, efficacy, references or programme novelty. Remove identifying pupil details from the research prompt.
- Deliver a self-contained English prompt and Dutch framing/handoff by default; honour English or other explicit language requests.
- Preserve focused questions in the main question/subquestions or explain a justified split. Do not substitute a fashionable topic for the intended change.
- Explore bounded adjacent fields with an explicit connection, as candidates rather than validated approaches.
- Request established and emerging evidence, contextual transfer, contrary/null findings, implementation conditions and reasoned confidence. Keep recent emphasis separate from an all-literature date cutoff.
- Distinguish intervention/framework/research method; distinguish wellbeing from learning outcomes, and knowledge/intentions from observed action where relevant.
- Ask for an evidence-linked landscape/shortlist without selecting it or filling an arbitrary quota; do not infer low uptake or novelty from little evidence.
- Hand off to user-run Elicit without requesting credentials, calling external research or claiming a completed report. Respect an explicitly chosen Elicit workflow.
- Keep EU policy, PRP, partners, budget, ToC and final concept choices outside this phase. On a returned-report fragment, acknowledge the next phase without fabricating its contents or automatically writing a ToC.

Any invented substantive fact or premature research claim is a failure. Missing context that is accurately retained as unknown is not. Readability targets and the suggested shortlist range are flexible, not hard pass/fail counts.

## Validation limits

Text simulations test instruction-following and prompt construction. They do not test Elicit's adherence to the prompt, scientific completeness, usefulness of real reports, voice UX, account access or grant quality. One user-run pilot with its report and source data is the next external validation step.
