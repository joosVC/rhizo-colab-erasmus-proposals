# Policy-screening evaluation

Give execution agents only the skill, raw user opening/turns and actual source artifacts required by the task. Keep this rubric, earlier reports and diagnoses out of execution context. Evaluate actual outputs and source calls separately. Do not run production writes or send identifying intake data during testing.

Before replaying the two Guide-only cases, supply the actual Guide 2026 v1 from the official register or the user's document, extract its text, and substitute its real path for `{guide_text_path}` in the case opening. The initial executions used the supplied Guide at a temporary workspace path; that file is not embedded in this repository. Check version identity before substitution. Do not replace it with a made-up summary.

## Behaviour and evidence checks

- Reuse available start input; require no Elicit report, solution, consortium or ToC. Ask only for genuinely missing input.
- Honour Dutch conversation/summary plus English report defaults and explicit all-English requests.
- Perform real browsing for live cases; consult relevant primary passages, not only snippets. Record read versus merely discovered sources. Use dates relative to the actual research date.
- Distinguish operative Guide/call/AWP, amendments, general Commission Work Programme and national agency context. Never equate a manifest entry with today's verified text.
- Preserve pinned supplied versions and explain current/future-year limitations beside judgments; never turn 2026 into confirmed 2027 call fit.
- Respect domain/action scope; distinguish a HEI participant from a KA210-HED field route. Do not provide invented award points or funding probabilities.
- Separate formal alignment, European need, policy momentum and possible contribution, with reasons, evidence confidence and Unknown where necessary.
- Keep proposals/consultations separate from adoption and implementation. Publication date differs from measurement/adoption/application date; repeated news is not independent corroboration.
- Retain uncertainty and local attribution. EU figures are not the school's prevalence. Do not invent causes, solutions, national priority boosts, novelty or missing practice materials.
- Provide traceable source links and exact relevant section/page/paragraph locators. A named unread amendment limits relevant conclusions; missing retrieval must not be hidden.
- Keep a bounded search and report coverage. Document-only cases must not claim live policy monitoring; unavailable call text prevents confirmed formal Strong fit.
- Offer justified options and questions for later human choice; no automatic ToC, PRP, partner selection or publication of the dossier.

## Failure conditions

Treat unsupported formal rules/status, invented citations/figures, concealed baseline gaps, fabricated research execution, future-year certainty and claimed grant scores as material failures. A partial report with explicit source gaps is acceptable. Length and number of opportunities are flexible.

## External validation boundary

Live synthetic cases test browsing and grounded report construction on current sources. They do not prove exhaustive policy coverage, legal review, future call correctness, real-colleague usability, installation or grant success. Offline cases use an actual supplied Guide text or explicitly synthetic fixtures; do not publish fixtures as EU facts.
